# Title

Grant Allocation for Headstarter’s Memejob Meme Launch Platform

# Abstract

This proposal seeks approval for a **588,235 CLXY** (~$25K) grant allocation to Headstarter for the development and marketing of their Memejob meme launch platform. The funding will directly support Headstarter's efforts to provide a socially-focused, retail-facing tool for the Hedera ecosystem. The grant aligns with The Creators Galaxy Foundation’s mission to foster 
community engagement and ecosystem growth.

# Motivation

The Creators Galaxy Foundation (TCG) is committed to empowering socially-oriented initiatives that bolster the Hedera ecosystem. This grant proposal targets a niche—meme-centric platforms—which currently lacks dedicated support. The Memejob platform presents an opportunity to address this gap by driving retail ecosystem growth, fostering innovation, and creating a vibrant and interactive community space.

# Rationale

Supporting Headstarter's Memejob platform is a strategic decision for TCG. The platform will:

* Strengthen Hedera's position as a retail ecosystem hub.
* Inspire developers to approach TCG for funding, enriching the ecosystem.
* Position TCG as a key partner in the success of Hedera-focused retail applications.
* Highlight TCG’s commitment to community-driven growth, ensuring broader adoption and long-term sustainability.

# User stories

1. **For Retail Users:** Memejob offers an engaging platform to discover and participate in meme-centric projects, encouraging interaction and innovation.
2. **For Developers:** Memejob provides an accessible launchpad for showcasing creativity, gaining visibility, and accessing community incentives.
3. **For TCG:** The partnership reinforces its role as a retail innovator and collaborator, fostering a stronger Hedera community.

# Specification

## Grant Allocation Breakdown
1. **$15K CLXY**: Development and platform enhancement.
   * Improving UI/UX for seamless user engagement.
   * Onboarding designers for platform aesthetics.
   * Adding innovative features and optimizing performance.
   * Building a scalable launchpad model that evolves with the community's needs.
2. **$10K CLXY**: Incentives, giveaways, and airdrops.
   * Attracting projects to launch their worlds on Calaxy.
   * Engaging the community with vibrant incentives.
   * Driving adoption through targeted campaigns.
## Expected Outcomes
1. **Strategic Partnership:**
   * TCG will be highlighted as a critical partner on Headstarter's platform and website.
   * Joint announcements will position TCG as a leader in Hedera retail initiatives.
   * Headerstarter will include a “Calaxy World Link”option on the Memejob platform for a trial period of 3 months from launch of the partnership. 
2. **Community Impact:**
   * Build momentum within the Hedera ecosystem by funding tangible retail applications.
   * Inspire developers to apply for TCG grants.
   * Establish TCG as a retail-focused foundation pivotal to Hedera’s growth.
3. **Sustainability Clause:**
   
   Headstarter is able to sell the grant tokens up to 10% of allocation once in a 30-day period, abiding by the below requirements:
     * **Maximum sale limit (the “Sale Limit”) per Transaction:** Project can not sell more than 0.1% of the company token circulating supply in a single transaction.
     * **Market Impact Restriction:** Any sales by the Project are to be executed in a manner that does not affect the market price of the token by more than 2% within any one-week period.
     * **Transaction Monitoring:** Transactions made by the Project will be monitored for compliance with this clause. Any transaction violating these conditions will be flagged and subject to potential reversal or other corrective actions.
     * **Reporting:** Project must report planned transactions to the Company at least 72 hours in advance for transactions involving more than 0.1% of the Circulating Supply

# Steps to Implement

1. Council Approves this proposal and allocate 588,235 CLXY (~$25K)  to Headstarter
2. Formalize a grant agreement with Headstarter, outlining usage expectations and deliverables.
3. Prepare joint announcements to amplify community engagement.
4. Begin disbursement of funds in two tranches:
   * ~$15K CLXY for development (352,941 CLXY)
   * ~$10K CLXY for marketing and incentives (235,294 CLXY)
